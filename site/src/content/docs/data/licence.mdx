## Background information

Before you use public data, you need to know the legal terms for reusing and sharing it. This is the **R** (Reusable) in FAIR.

Normally, you check the licence before you download anything. In this lab you started the download first to save time, so read the licence while the data downloads.

## To do

<Steps>

1. Go to the [NSCLC-Radiomics collection page](https://www.cancerimagingarchive.net/collection/nsclc-radiomics/).
2. Scroll down to the **Data Access** table and find the **License** column:

   <a href={dataAccess.src} target="_blank">
   	<Image src={dataAccess} alt="The Data Access table of the NSCLC-Radiomics collection page, with the License column outlined in red. The table has two rows: the images, segmentations and radiation therapy structures (DICOM), and the Lung1 clinical data (CSV)." />
   </a>

   Click the image to see it at full size.

3. Read the **Citations & Data Usage Policy** section.
4. Discuss these questions with your partner and write down your answers:

   - Under which licence is this dataset published (for example Creative Commons BY 3.0 or 4.0)?
   - Does the licence allow commercial reuse, such as building and selling a diagnostic AI application?
   - What exactly must you do to meet the attribution requirement when you publish results?

</Steps>
