## Background information

The manifest is a `.tcia` file that doesn't contain any images. It is a list of pointers to the image series you selected, which the download tool uses to fetch all of them in one batch.

## To do

<Steps>

1. Click **Add to Cart** above the results table.
2. Click the **Cart** icon in the top-right corner and check that it contains only the 3 to 5 patients you selected. The cart lists every image series of those patients, so you will see each patient more than once. It can look like this, including a warning:

   ![The TCIA cart with series of patients LUNG1-001, LUNG1-026, LUNG1-060 and LUNG1-104, and a warning: "You have series with commercial use restrictions. Your cart contains 15 series with commercial restrictions. There is a filter available to exclude restricted series." The buttons are Proceed and Disable Commercial Series.](../../../assets/tcia-cart-warning.png)

   Some series in the cart are segmentations (the **Segmentation** rows). You won't use them in this lab, but you can still download them and explore them if there is time at the end.

3. Discuss with your partner and write down your answer: if you see a warning like this, how would you proceed?
4. Before you download the data, click **Export spreadsheet** (top right of the cart, next to **Download**) to save a list of all the series in your cart. Open the file, for example in Excel, and inspect it with your partner:

   - What information does the file give about each series?
   - How many series does each patient have, and what kind of series are they?
   - Which of this information could be useful later, when you process the images?

5. Click **Download** to get the manifest file (`.tcia`).

</Steps>
